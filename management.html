General

Automatic updates are checked in the background at startup and every hour. A completed update restarts the monitor with the same arguments. Port 8765 is fixed. An IP change takes effect after restarting the Python monitor. Selecting 0.0.0.0 enables the full password-protected dashboard through this machine's LAN or public IP. Direct public-IP access uses unencrypted HTTP; prefer a trusted VPN or an HTTPS reverse proxy.
